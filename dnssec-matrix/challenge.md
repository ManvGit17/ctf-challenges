## Hints

The hints are ROT13 encoded so you don't read one by accident. Decode only what you need, in
order. Any ROT13 decoder works, for example [rot13.com](https://rot13.com) or CyberChef.

1. `Na rzcgl nafjre qbrf abg zrna gur freire unf abguvat gb fnl. Purpx jung nppbzcnavrf aba-rkvfgrapr.`
2. `Jvyqpneqf snvy, ohg gurve qravny erirnyf ubj gur mbar unfurf vgf obeqref.`
3. `Punva rnpu unfu jvgu vgf fnyg naq vgrengvbaf hagvy gur zngevk grezvangrf ng gur frperg abqr.`
